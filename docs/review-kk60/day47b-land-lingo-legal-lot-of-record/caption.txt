📖 LAND LINGO: Legal Lot of Record

A parcel that was lawfully created under the rules in effect at the time. Some old tax lots were never legally divided — and carry no right to build.

Why it matters: A tax lot number is not proof you can build. On odd parcels, get a legal-lot determination first.
