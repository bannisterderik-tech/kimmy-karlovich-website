📖 LAND LINGO: Easement

A recorded legal right for someone else to use part of your land — or for you to cross someone else's. Roads, utilities, and irrigation ditches all travel on easements.

Why it matters: "We've always used that road" is not an easement. If your access isn't recorded, you don't have access.
