📖 LAND LINGO: EFU

Exclusive Farm Use — Oregon's agricultural zoning. It protects farm ground, but it also limits what you can build. A new home on EFU land usually has to qualify through a farm-income test or a specific approval path.

Why it matters: That gorgeous 40 acres might not allow a house. Ever. Verify with county planning before you fall for it.
