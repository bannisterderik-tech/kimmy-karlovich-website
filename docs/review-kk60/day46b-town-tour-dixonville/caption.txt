🚜 TOWN TOUR, stop 17 of 26: DIXONVILLE

East county pasture country. Dixonville spreads across the hills east of Roseburg along Highway 138 — a patchwork of cattle pastures, orchards, and homesteads on the way to the North Umpqua. Close-in but unmistakably country.

Property-wise: Rolling pasture with southern exposure, small orchards, and shop properties. Popular with buyers who want acreage on the "river side" of town.
