Canyonville, briefly.

Canyonville sits at the mouth of Canyon Creek where the South Umpqua bends through steep, forested canyon country. Seven Feathers Casino Resort anchors the local economy, and the surrounding hills hold everything from mini-ranches to serious off-grid retreats.

What inspections do I need for a home on well and septic?

At minimum: well flow test, water quality (bacteria, nitrates, arsenic), septic inspection with tank pumping, plus the standard home inspection. In Douglas County, add a woodstove check and a wildfire-insurance availability check. Kimmy schedules all of it inside your inspection window.

Is rural homeowners insurance harder to get now?

In some fire-prone areas, yes — carriers look at fire district response, defensible space, and roof type. Kimmy recommends getting an insurance quote during the inspection period, not after, and can point you to brokers who know rural Douglas County.

How fast is internet in rural Douglas County?

It varies from fiber (yes, some valleys have it) to DSL to Starlink-only. Remote workers should verify service at the specific address before waiving contingencies — Kimmy keeps current notes on which roads have which providers.
