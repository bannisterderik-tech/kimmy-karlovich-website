📖 LAND LINGO: Farm Deferral

Oregon's special assessment that taxes qualifying farm ground at its farm-use value instead of market value — often a fraction of the normal bill.

Why it matters: Lose the qualifying use and the county can claw back years of back taxes. Know what you're inheriting before you close.
