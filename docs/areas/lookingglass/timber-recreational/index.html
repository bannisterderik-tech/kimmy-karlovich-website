How do I know what the timber is worth?

A timber cruise — a professional inventory of species, volume, and grade — is the only reliable answer. On any significant timbered parcel, Kimmy recommends cruising before setting or accepting a price, and she can connect you with reputable local foresters.

What is forest tax deferral?

Like farm deferral, forestland deferral taxes ground at forest-use value — dramatically lower than market. Continued forest use is required, and disqualification triggers back taxes, so confirm status and obligations before closing.

If I log my land, do I have to replant?

Yes — the Oregon Forest Practices Act requires reforestation after harvest, typically within two planting seasons, plus stream buffer protections. A prior owner's unfinished replant obligation can transfer with the land, so Kimmy checks harvest notifications before closing.
