Idleyld Park, Oregon

Idleyld Park: North Umpqua wild and scenic.

Idleyld Park is the last community before the North Umpqua goes fully wild — emerald water, basalt canyons, and the Rogue-Umpqua Scenic Byway climbing toward Diamond Lake and Crater Lake. Cabins and river homes here are heirloom properties.

Riverfront cabins, forested view parcels, and recreation basecamps. Post-fire landscape recovery, insurance, and septic siting near the river are the key diligence items.
