📖 LAND LINGO: Priority Date

The seniority ranking of a water right. In a dry August, the Watermaster shuts off junior (newer) rights first.

Why it matters: An 1890s right on the South Umpqua is a genuinely valuable asset. A 1990s right on the same stretch may go dry when you need it most.
