Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to an accessible website — and how to reach us if something isn't working for you.

Our commitment

Everyone deserves full access to information about buying and selling property. This site is built to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as closely as practical, and accessibility is considered in every update.

What we've done

Semantic HTML structure with proper heading order and landmarks; descriptive alt text on meaningful images; sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds; keyboard-navigable menus and controls; readable font sizes with relative units that respect browser zoom; reduced-motion support that disables animations when your system requests it; and forms with associated labels.

Known limitations

Some photography-heavy sections are decorative by nature, and third-party services we link to (Instagram, Zillow) have their own accessibility practices we can't control.

Alternative access

Every piece of information on this site is available directly from Kimmy by phone or email — often faster than any website. If a page isn't working with your assistive technology, call 541-643-9509 and you'll get the same information, human to human.

Feedback

If you hit an accessibility barrier anywhere on this site, please tell us: kimberly@theoperativegroup.com or 541-643-9509. Reports get fixed, not filed. Last reviewed August 2026.
