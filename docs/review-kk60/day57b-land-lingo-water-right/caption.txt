📖 LAND LINGO: Water Right

A license to use public water — attached to specific land, for a specific use, with a specific seniority. In Oregon: first in time, first in right, use it or lose it.

Why it matters: Five years of non-use can forfeit a right forever. Green pasture in the photos means nothing without the paper behind it.
