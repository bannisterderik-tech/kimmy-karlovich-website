🚜 TOWN TOUR, stop 18 of 26: TENMILE

Quiet ranch country. Tenmile lies southwest of Roseburg on the road to Camas Valley — open valley ranchland around Tenmile Creek, with Olalla Reservoir in the hills above. It's unhurried, affordable, and authentically…

Property-wise: Cattle and hay operations, older farmhouses with good bones, and timbered upland parcels. Excellent value per acre compared to close-in valleys.
