📖 LAND LINGO: GPM

How well output is measured. A well's sustained GPM — not the first burst from the pressure tank — tells you whether a property can support a household, a garden, and stock.

Why it matters: 5+ GPM sustained is comfortable for a home. Below 3, you're planning around storage tanks.
