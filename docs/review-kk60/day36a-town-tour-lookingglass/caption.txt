🚜 TOWN TOUR, stop 16 of 26: LOOKINGGLASS

The valley that shines. Named when early settlers said its grass shimmered like a looking glass, this valley southwest of Roseburg is postcard Oregon: green pastures, white fences, and the Lookingglass Store still pouring…

Property-wise: Premier horse and cattle ground — flat, fertile, and close to town. Properties here are tightly held and often sell through word of mouth before they list.
