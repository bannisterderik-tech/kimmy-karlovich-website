Is the Umpqua Valley really good wine country?

Yes — it's one of Oregon's oldest AVAs, with the state's first post-Prohibition vinifera plantings, and its warm valleys ripen varieties the Willamette can't. The Elkton sub-AVA produces standout cool-climate Pinot Noir and Riesling.

How much does it cost to plant a vineyard?

Plan on roughly $15,000–$30,000+ per acre to establish vines (site prep, plants, trellis, irrigation) before your first real crop in year three or four. Kimmy helps buyers find ground where the site quality justifies the investment.

Can I have a tasting room or events on my property?

Oregon land-use law gives wineries meaningful allowances on farm-zoned land, but specifics depend on production and county rules. Kimmy coordinates with Douglas County Planning to confirm what a given property can host.
